# No voiceprints, ever

The app never stores any representation of a person's voice beyond the current Session — no speaker embeddings, no enrollment, no cross-Session voice memory, local or cloud. Within-Session diarization (clustering voices inside one call, discarded after) is fine; persistent voice profiles are not. This is a permanent line, not a v1 deferral: cross-meeting voiceprint reuse is exactly what the consolidated Otter.ai class action (N.D. Cal., 2025) attacks, and under the Australian Privacy Act biometric templates used for identification are "sensitive information" requiring consent that call participants have not given. Speaker names come from the user's Participants hints and manual edits instead.

## Consequences

- Cloud speaker-identification/enrollment APIs are off-limits regardless of provider.
- "Remember this colleague's voice" must be declined as a feature request; the convenience it offers is precisely the litigated behaviour.
