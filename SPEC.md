# Braid
status: draft
owner: Alexandro
one-liner: A native macOS menu-bar app that records calls as two Tracks, transcribes and identifies speakers on-device, summarises on-device, and writes markdown Notes into an Obsidian Vault. Nothing it hears ever leaves the Mac.

## Outcome
Problem: meeting knowledge evaporates after calls; manual note-taking during them is lossy and distracting; and the people you meet weekly arrive as anonymous "Speaker 1" every single time.
User: the owner, on an 8GB M3 MacBook Air already strained by Teams.
Success measure (all owner-verified, all can fail):
1. With Immediate Delivery, a Note appears in the Vault within 15 minutes of Stop with zero manual steps, for ≥9 of the first 10 real calls.
2. After 2 weeks of use, the owner has stopped taking manual notes during calls.
3. A Person named once is recognised on return: across the first month, at least 7 of every 10 returning Speakers with ≥2 minutes of speech are auto-named, and **every** auto-assigned name is correct — one wrong auto-name fails this measure outright.

## The Journey
1. One-time setup: choose the Vault folder, review the four Presets, maintain the Key Terms list. No accounts, no API keys. Selecting the Parakeet Engine later downloads its models; everything else is OS-provided.
2. Call starts → click the menu-bar icon → Start. The popover offers: Preset (default Meeting), optional Title (defaults to the Preset name), optional Participants, and an optional asserted speaker count (with a strict variant that also caps it). Recording begins: two Tracks, crash-safe on disk, continuously flushed.
3. During the call: the icon shows recording state; Pause/Resume any time; a pause stops both Tracks on the shared clock and is marked in the Transcript, never silently joined. No inference of any kind runs while any Session is recording.
4. Call ends → Stop. A Job is created and runs entirely on this Mac.
5. The Job transcribes each Track with the chosen Engine (Mic Track is "Me" structurally, no diarization) and diarizes the Remote Track. Each Speaker's representative embedding is matched against the Voice Database: confident matches are auto-named; plausible matches become suggestions; Speakers matching Me's Voiceprint are folded as echo. Re-attribution then corrects stretches the Voiceprints confidently contradict.
6. For every Speaker still unnamed, a Voice Clip is extracted before anything is deleted. If Delivery is Held and unnamed Speakers remain, the Job pauses before summarising and waits indefinitely, visibly pending in the panel. With Immediate Delivery (default), processing continues with generic labels.
7. The Summariser produces the Note on-device from the Transcript using the Preset; long Transcripts are summarised map-reduce. The Note is written to the Vault, the Transcript to `<Vault>/transcripts/`; the Recording is deleted only after both are confirmed on disk.
8. macOS notification: "Note ready", and "N voices to name" when unnamed Speakers exist. The naming flow, in the one window: each unnamed Speaker with a playable Voice Clip, how long they spoke, suggestion chips (voice matches first, then Participants, then recent Persons), a free text field, and Skip. Naming relabels the Transcript, re-summarises, rewrites Note and Transcript in place (R6a), and enrolls the confirmed Voiceprint; the Session's Voice Clips are deleted once its Identification resolves.
9. On any failure the Recording is kept and the icon shows the error state. A Job blocked by a temporary local condition (a Session recording, Engine assets not yet installed) waits and resumes by itself; anything else parks for user-initiated Retry.

## Non-goals
1. No live transcription or scrollback UI.
2. No auto-detection of call start; Start is always manual (auto-stop per R17).
3. No calendar, EventKit, Graph, or Teams integration — ever. Identity comes from voices and the owner's naming, never from an external source.
4. No cloud services: no STT API, no LLM API, no telemetry, no sync. Network I/O exists only to fetch Engine model assets ([ADR-0006](docs/adr/0006-zero-cloud.md)).
5. No inference of any kind while recording; R4's in-call budget is what the 8GB constraint protects.
6. No true clusterer seeding in this version: known voices are never fed into the diarizer's clustering (Re-attribution corrects its output instead); recorded as a future direction pending upstream support.
7. No distribution: personal build, one Mac, no notarization, no App Store.
8. No in-app transcript or note viewer/editor; Notes are read and fixed in Obsidian.
9. No RAG or chat over Notes.

## Language
The project's language lives in [CONTEXT.md](./CONTEXT.md); its terms are binding. To read this spec you need: **Session** (one Start→Stop span), **Track** (Mic = "Me", Remote = everyone else), **Speaker** (one distinct voice in a Session's Remote Track), **Person** / **Voiceprint** / **Voice Database** (a durable named identity / one stored exemplar of its voice / the encrypted store of them), **Identification** / **Enrollment** (resolving a Speaker to a Person / the learning that naming causes), **Engine** (an on-device transcription implementation), **Delivery** (Immediate or Held), **Job** (the post-Stop pipeline).

## Design
Native, minimal, no web frontend of any kind. AppKit owns the status item and the one window; SwiftUI draws its content, with no `@State` anywhere (ADR-0004).

**One window.** The app never puts a second thing on screen. The Sessions panel hangs from the menu bar icon, and every part of the app is a view inside it: Session history, the live recording block, naming, settings, and confirmations. Left click opens and closes it; right click gives shortcuts plus Quit. macOS notifications are the only surface while the panel is closed. The panel is dark regardless of system appearance.

**The naming flow** is one panel view: one row per unnamed Speaker — a play button for its Voice Clip, how long they spoke ("14 min, 32 turns"), suggestion chips in order (voice match "Sounds like Sarah?", then that Session's Participants, then recently named Persons), a free text field, and Skip. Naming is one tap on a chip or a few keystrokes. **Settings** gains: the Delivery toggle ("Wait for speaker names before writing the Note"), the Engine picker, and the Persons list (rename / forget / delete all / export / import). Presets, the Key Terms editor, and the call-app watch list keep their existing Settings homes.

Concurrency: one Session at a time; Jobs run in the background, so Start is always available. Icon precedence: recording/paused > error > processing > idle.

## Architecture
Swift, menu-bar-only (`LSUIElement`), SwiftPM + Command Line Tools, macOS 27, Swift Concurrency throughout ([ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-swiftpm-no-xcode-toolchain.md)). One third-party dependency: FluidAudio, pinned exactly — the sole exception to ADR-0004's zero-dependency policy, unchanged by this revision.

**Capture** ([ADR-0001](docs/adr/0001-process-tap-two-track-capture.md)): Core Audio process tap + physical mic in one aggregate device with drift compensation. Each Track is a mono CAF, 16kHz 16-bit PCM, continuously flushed. No transcode exists: Engines read the CAF originals.

**Transcription** ([ADR-0005](docs/adr/0005-local-transcription-as-a-provider.md), [ADR-0006](docs/adr/0006-zero-cloud.md)): two Engines behind the `TranscriberEngine` protocol — Apple `SpeechTranscriber` (default: better measured attribution, Key Terms as `contextualStrings`, no download) and Parakeet TDT via FluidAudio (better WER, models cached under Application Support). Diarization: FluidAudio's offline pyannote community-1 pipeline, Remote Track only. `SpeakerAligner` (R22) then the merge rule, both unchanged.

**Identification**: the diarizer result's per-cluster centroids are matched against Voiceprints by cosine similarity; two thresholds (suggest, auto), the re-attribution margin, and the Voiceprint cap live in one config file, calibrated via the harness. Re-attribution (R27) uses the pipeline's per-chunk embeddings (`exposeChunkEmbeddings`) to correct cluster contamination against enrolled Persons. Raw embeddings never cross the Job boundary (R21). **The measurement harness** is the app binary's `--local-check <audio> [--reference <json>] [--engine ...]` mode: it scores WER, coverage, turn purity, and identification precision/recall against labelled fixtures in `test-audio/` (real recordings, gitignored), printing aggregates and short excerpts only, never writing a transcript to disk.

**Voice Database**: one file under `~/Library/Application Support/Braid/`, ChaChaPoly-encrypted (CryptoKit), key in the Keychain (this-device-only). Inside: schema version, embedding model version, Persons `{id, name, voiceprints: [{vector, heardAt, seconds}]}`. Dozens of Persons, kilobytes each; no database engine.

**Summarisation**: Apple Foundation Models on-device, guided generation emitting each Preset's sections as typed structures; map-reduce for Transcripts beyond the context window. The Summariser sits behind a protocol seam so a stronger local engine can replace it on evidence. Preset defaults live in the repo, seeded at first launch, user-editable; all four embed the R11 canonical rule.

**Merge rule**: both Tracks share the Recording clock; the Transcript interleaves both utterance lists by start timestamp. Remote Speakers numbered by first appearance; every Mic utterance is "Me"; pause markers at their clock position.

**Transcript format**: markdown, one line per utterance: `- **HH:MM:SS Speaker:** text`, timestamps in elapsed recorded-audio time since Start (the clock pauses with the Recording); pause markers as their own line.

**Vault delivery**: atomic write (temp file, rename). Transcript at `<Vault>/transcripts/<Note name> (transcript).md`, linked from the Note's frontmatter as a wikilink.

**State**: Job state and pending Recordings in `~/Library/Application Support/Braid/`; Voice Clips under it too, transient per R25. Signing: the stable self-signed identity "ms-notes Development" (ADR-0004; renaming it makes TCC forget granted permissions).

## Operation
No server, no accounts, no spend. Production = the signed `.app` in `/Applications`, launched at login. Deploy = build script → copy; rollback = reinstall the previous version's kept zip; one git tag per installed version. Logging = unified logging (`os_log`); the pipeline category records each Job step, including per-Job Re-attribution corrections; every failure also surfaces in the menu-bar error state. Model assets: Apple speech and foundation-model assets are OS-owned; Parakeet models (443MB) are fetched only if that Engine is selected. Backups: Notes and Transcripts live in the Vault under its existing backup; the Voice Database is included in Time Machine as an encrypted blob restorable only onto this Mac (the key never leaves the Keychain) — export (R29) is the deliberate cross-machine path; Recordings are transient by design. Data protection: nothing derived from audio leaves the machine, ever (R20, R21); deletion rights are first-class (R29). Consent posture: the owner verbally mentions recording; local-only processing and audio auto-deletion are the mitigations.

## Done
Owner-executed (the R-checks are agent-runnable; this gate is human). On the owner's Mac, in production, **with Wi-Fi off**: record one real call of ≥15 minutes with ≥2 remote speakers, using Pause once and entering Participants. Within 15 minutes of Stop, hands-off: a Note is in the Vault with the pause marked, no name guessed, frontmatter complete, Transcript written and linked, Recording deleted. Name the Speakers through the naming flow, hearing each Voice Clip; the Note is rewritten named and the clips are gone. Then, within the following week, a Session containing one of those named Persons auto-names them with zero interaction — and no Session in the first month ever auto-names anyone wrongly. Then the rolling criterion: ≥9 of the first 10 real calls deliver their Note hands-off within 15 minutes. Anything less fails the spec.
